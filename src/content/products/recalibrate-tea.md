## The blend

Ginkgo Biloba, Gotu Kola, Brahmi, Herb Robert, Tulsi (Holy Basil), Cornflower, Moringa, L-theanine. Organically grown, sourced across Australia and traditional regions.

## How to brew

**Mild:** 1 tsp, water at 90 °C, cover and steep 3–5 minutes.

**Strong:** 2 tablespoons, water at 90 °C, cover and steep 5–10 minutes.

Drink 1–2 cups across the morning. Add raw honey if desired.

## What users report

Traditional use suggests support for memory, concentration, and cerebral blood flow. Reported effects vary.
